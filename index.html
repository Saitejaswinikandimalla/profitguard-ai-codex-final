Methodological Transparency

This is a synthetic educational dataset. The audit flags 4,770 below-cost orders across 76 SKUs; that signal does not prove the price was erroneous or unplanned.

Negative gross margin is an estimate from the provided unit price, discount, quantity, and product cost. The prototype does not connect to a checkout system or prove recoverability.

UNIT MARGIN FORMULA:
Estimated Margin = Quantity × [ Unit_Price × (1 - Discount_Pct) - Unit_Cost ]
